A few things
you might wonder.

What if my child needs a little time?

That’s why we allow 25 minutes for a 15-minute session. We begin gently, with playful prompts and time to get comfortable. Expressions will naturally vary from child to child.

What should they wear?

Simple, comfortable clothes keep the attention on their face. Soft neutrals and subtle textures work beautifully; avoid large logos or busy patterns.

Can I book for siblings?

Yes. Each child has a separate appointment so they can have our full attention. A sibling Mini is $175 + tax. Contact us to arrange consecutive times if they aren’t available online.

Is a framed print included?

Each collection includes a digital collage, ready for printing. Printed and framed artwork is separate, with pricing shared before purchase. All Expressions includes a $50 print credit.

When will I get the photos?

Two sneak peek images arrive within 24 hours. Your full private gallery and digital collage are delivered in 5 business days.

How do booking and the deposit work?

Once dates are released, choose your collection, select a time in the scheduler, review the policies, and pay a 50% deposit. The checkout will show applicable tax and when the remaining balance is due. Cancellation and rescheduling terms will be provided before payment.
