A few things
you might wonder.

What is a Little Expressions session?

A playful 15-minute children’s portrait session for ages 3–10, with individual portraits, a digital collage, two sneak peeks within 24 hours, and a private gallery within five business days. The Mini collection includes 3 expression portraits; other collections include more.

What if my child needs a little time?

That’s okay. We set aside 25 minutes for the visit so your child has time to get comfortable before we take their portraits.

What should my child wear for their photos?

Simple, comfortable clothes keep the attention on their face. Soft neutrals and subtle textures work beautifully; avoid large logos or busy patterns.

Can I book for siblings?

Yes! Choose two or three children in the booking form and one collection per child. We photograph each child individually, then take sibling portraits together at no extra charge. Each child receives their own digital collage. For sibling portraits together only, choose a one-child appointment and select the siblings-together option.

Is a framed print included?

Each collection includes a digital collage, ready for printing. Printed and framed artwork is separate, with pricing shared before purchase. All Expressions includes a $50 print credit.

When will I get my child’s photos?

We’ll send two sneak peeks within 24 hours. You’ll get your full private gallery and digital collage within five business days.

Where is the session?

Sessions take place at 124 Blanco Drive, Hutto, TX — a short drive from Austin. Your confirmation email includes directions and what to bring.

How do booking and payment work?

Little Expressions is paid in full at booking — nothing is due on the session day. Card payments include applicable Texas sales tax and processing fees; the exact total is shown in Calendly before payment.
