Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Do you photograph on location at our office?
Yes. We come to you anywhere in Austin and Central Texas, set up quickly, and work efficiently so your team keeps its day. A clean studio-style backdrop can be brought on location when you want a consistent look.
How quickly are images delivered?
Fully edited images are typically delivered within five business days, sized for both web and print.
Can headshots be matched across an entire team?
Yes. We keep lighting, background, and finish consistent across every person — whether it's three people or thirty — so your website, press kit, and annual report look cohesive.
Are the images ready for LinkedIn and press?
Yes. Every image is delivered in web and print sizes, ready for LinkedIn, websites, press kits, and speaker bios.
How do we book a session?
Check availability and reserve your date, and we'll follow up to plan your space, your look, and the timing.
