Good to Know

Frequently Asked

What does quinceañera coverage include?
A formal portrait session beforehand and full coverage of the day itself — the Misa de acción de gracias if there is one, the court, the vals, and the celebration that follows.
Do you photograph the church Mass?
Yes, and we do it reverently and unobtrusively. For Catholic families, you can also read more on our Catholic Photography pages about how we work during Mass.
Can we do portraits on a separate day?
Absolutely. A dedicated portrait session — in your dress, at a setting you love — gives you timeless images without the rush of the celebration day.
¿Hablan español?
Sí. We're happy to plan your day in English or Spanish, whichever is easiest for your family.
When will we receive the photos?
A sneak peek follows soon after the celebration, with the full edited gallery delivered a few weeks later.
