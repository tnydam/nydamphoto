Questions

Questions About Senior Collections

Which senior portrait collection should we choose?
It depends on how much variety your senior wants. The Post suits a focused, classic session. The Frame fits seniors who want more outfits, locations, or personality woven in. The Cover Story is for a fully styled, complete experience.
When should we book senior portraits?
Most families book in spring or summer of junior year through early senior year, which gives the most flexibility on dates and locations.
Do you help with posing?
Yes. Every collection includes guided posing and direction, so your senior is never left wondering what to do.
How many photos will we get?
The Post includes 50 photos to choose from with 25 fully edited. The Frame includes 100 photos to choose from with 60 fully edited. The Cover Story includes 150 photos to choose from with 80 fully edited.
How many outfits should my senior bring?
The Cover Story allows time for multiple planned looks. We help prioritize outfits so the session remains relaxed and every look receives enough time.
Can we include dance, sports, music, or hobbies?
Yes, and we encourage it. A senior's interests can be woven into The Frame and The Cover Story sessions without making the day feel like a theme shoot.
Do parents help choose final images?
Yes. Parents are welcome in the gallery review process, and we're happy to talk through favorites together.
When will we receive our images?
You'll receive a few sneak peek images within 24 hours. Your full proof gallery is typically ready within 7–10 business days, and after you select the images included in your collection, the fully edited final gallery is usually delivered within another 10–14 business days.
Do you offer prints or albums?
Yes. Personal printing rights are included with every collection, and albums or fine art prints can be added.
What is the difference between a senior collection and The Senior Year Story?
Senior portrait collections are our standard, single-session senior photography options. The Senior Year Story is a separate year-long experience consisting of three real senior-year moments plus the complete Cover Story portrait session.
Do you photograph senior sessions outside Austin?
Yes. We photograph seniors throughout the greater Austin area and the Texas Hill Country, including Westlake, Bee Cave, Lakeway, Round Rock, Georgetown, Cedar Park, Leander, Dripping Springs and Hutto. We recommend locations based on the look and experience your senior wants.
Is payment due at booking?
Senior sessions are reserved with payment in full at booking unless otherwise arranged.
How much do senior portraits cost?
Our senior sessions begin at $500 for The Post, $750 for The Frame, and $950 for The Cover Story, all plus tax. Edited digital images are included in every session, with no required image purchase afterward.
Which collection is the most popular?
The Frame is our most chosen collection. It offers more variety and locations than The Post, giving families a fuller portrait story without the full scope of The Cover Story.
