What Is The Senior Year Story?

The Senior Year Story is a year-long photography experience for Class of 2027 families in Austin and the Texas Hill Country. We document three meaningful senior-year moments as they naturally happen — one in fall, winter and spring — then create the complete Cover Story portrait session with professional hair and makeup. It's not four separate photoshoots to plan; it's the year your family is already living, thoughtfully preserved.

What It Is

Most senior portraits happen the same way: one booking, one sitting, done. That's exactly what our Senior Collections are built for — a focused, beautiful session in a single day.

The Senior Year Story is different. Instead of one appointment, we document three real moments already on your family's calendar — a game, a recital, a morning at the barn — as they naturally happen, across fall, winter and spring. Then we bring it all together with one complete Cover Story portrait session.

This is not four photo sessions to plan. The three chapters happen at events you're already attending — you don't need to style another outfit, plan another shoot, or hold a phone all evening. The only dedicated portrait session is The Cover Story.

The Three Chapters

Fall, winter, and spring — one real moment each, captured as it happens. No posing, no styled production, no extra photoshoot to plan.

Chapter One · Fall

The Beginning

Friday night lights, the first day back, a last homecoming — whatever moment matters to your senior this fall. We come to it as it happens, observed naturally. Approximately 30–60 minutes.

Chapter Two · Winter

What Makes This Year Theirs

A morning at the barn, a recital, time with close friends — winter's quieter, closer moments, documented as they happen.

Chapter Three · Spring

The Lasts

Prom, college decisions, the countdown to graduation — spring is the last chance to capture senior year as it's actually being lived.

The Cover Story

The Cover Story is the complete portrait experience — two to three hours across up to three thoughtfully planned locations, with professional hair and makeup, multiple planned looks, and guided posing and direction throughout. By the time it arrives, your senior has already lived three chapters of their year in front of our camera, so it never feels like the first time.

The three chapters aren't extra sessions tacked on for value — they're what makes the Cover Story feel effortless when it arrives.

What's Included

  • Three chapters across fall, winter and spring
  • Approximately 30–60 minutes of coverage for each chosen moment
  • The complete Cover Story portrait session
  • Professional hair and makeup for the Cover Story
  • Planning and scheduling across the year
  • Edited digital images from all included chapters
  • Private galleries
  • Optional fine art album, prints and wall art

Pricing

The Senior Year Story is $1,650 + tax. Early enrollment is $1,350 + tax through August 31.

How to Reserve

Because this work requires our ongoing availability and personal attention throughout the year, we take on only a small number of year-long stories each year. The earlier you reach out, the better — inquire here to start the conversation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Senior Year Story?
A year-long photography experience for Class of 2027 families in Austin and the Texas Hill Country. We document three meaningful senior-year moments as they naturally happen — one in fall, winter and spring — then create the complete Cover Story portrait session with professional hair and makeup.
Is this four separate photo sessions?
No. The only traditional portrait session is The Cover Story. The three chapters happen at events already on your family's calendar, so they require little or no additional preparation.
How long is each chapter?
Approximately 30–60 minutes, depending on the event.
Can I book a single senior session instead?
Yes. Individual senior sessions are available separately through our Senior Collections, for families who'd prefer one session rather than The Senior Year Story.
What is included in The Cover Story session?
Two to three hours across up to three thoughtfully planned locations, multiple planned looks, professional hair and makeup, styling and session planning, guided posing and direction, 150 images to choose from with 80 fully edited, and optional artwork and album consultation.
How much does The Senior Year Story cost?
$1,650 plus tax. Early enrollment is $1,350 plus tax through August 31.
How far do you travel?
We photograph seniors throughout Austin and the Texas Hill Country, including Westlake, Bee Cave, Barton Creek, Lake Travis, Lakeway, Tarrytown, Georgetown, Cedar Park, Round Rock and Hutto.
When should we reserve the experience?
As early as possible. Because this work requires our ongoing availability and personal attention throughout the year, we take on only a small number of year-long stories each year.
