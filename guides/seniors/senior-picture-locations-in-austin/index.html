10 Austin-Area Locations for Senior Pictures

Start with how you want your senior pictures to feel. Compare city architecture, gardens, shaded trails and skyline views, then see what to wear, when to go and what to check before you get there.

Choose by the look you want

For fashion-inspired portraits, start with Domain NORTHSIDE or Laguna Gloria. For a classic cap-and-gown look, try the Capitol grounds. For an easy mix of water and walking portraits, start with Mueller Lake Park. If the landscape matters most, compare Mount Bonnell with Auditorium Shores.

01 / Modern editorial

Domain NORTHSIDE

Clean storefronts, straight lines and open plazas give portraits a fashion-editorial feel.

What to wear and try. Try a tailored jacket, polished denim or a monochrome outfit. Start in open shade and use a quiet frontage for walking frames.

When to go. Late afternoon on a weekday.

Access and planning. Private property. Ask management for written approval before planning a professional shoot. Venue information ↗

02 / Open-country portraits

Dreams Ranch

Open skies and a rustic farm setting make room for relaxed, wide portraits.

What to wear and try. Bring denim, boots or a dress that moves. Plan one wide frame with the landscape and a closer portrait with a quieter backdrop.

When to go. The last hour before sunset if the owner offers that time.

Access and planning. Confirm the exact property, directions, pricing and availability directly with the owner. Ask before planning around animals or props.

03 / Shaded paths

Brightwater Park

Pathways and greenery work well for casual portraits with movement.

What to wear and try. Try an everyday outfit and shoes you can walk in. Use shaded sections for close-ups and a clear path for full-length frames.

When to go. Morning or late afternoon.

Access and planning. Use the main entrance at 8601 Brightwater Blvd in Round Rock. Confirm restroom access with park management before counting on it for changes. Venue information ↗

04 / Hilltop views

Mount Bonnell

The stone stairway and an overlook above Lake Austin offer strong backdrops for wide portraits.

What to wear and try. Arrive dressed in your first outfit and bring walking shoes. Keep accessories simple so the landscape has room in the frame.

When to go. The last hour before sunset, within your permit window.

Access and planning. City photo permit required for posed portraits. Parking is at the base and the overlook involves stairs. Venue information ↗

05 / Villa and gardens

Laguna Gloria

The Driscoll Villa, sculpture grounds and lakeside setting suit a more refined portrait style.

What to wear and try. Try a dress or tailored look in a soft solid color. Let the villa or greenery frame the portrait and check rules before including artwork.

When to go. Choose from the venue’s appointment windows.

Access and planning. Advance booking required. Standard personal portraits are listed at $150 per hour. Confirm availability and ask about separate approval for styled or fashion work. Venue information ↗

06 / Garden portraits

Mayfield Park

Paths, lily ponds and a historic cottage create an intimate garden setting.

What to wear and try. Try soft colors or understated florals. Start near the cottage and use a quieter path for walking frames. Treat peacocks as a bonus.

When to go. Late afternoon in softer light.

Access and planning. City photo permit required for posed portraits. Allow extra time for limited parking and keep pathways clear. Venue information ↗

07 / Classic architecture

Texas State Capitol Grounds

Pink granite and formal walkways give cap-and-gown portraits a clean, recognizable Austin backdrop.

What to wear and try. Wear a solid tone that stands out against pale stone. Use the building for a wide frame and a quieter walkway for close-ups.

When to go. Early morning during public hours.

Access and planning. Check grounds hours and closures. Confirm the rules for your planned shoot with the State Preservation Board. Venue information ↗

08 / Austin skyline

Auditorium Shores

Open lawn and the view across Lady Bird Lake work well for movement and wide skyline portraits.

What to wear and try. Choose a color that stands out against grass and sky. Start with closer portraits and save the wide skyline frame for softer light.

When to go. Golden hour, within your reserved window.

Access and planning. City photo permit required for posed portraits. Check event closures and allow time to get from parking to the lawn. Venue information ↗

09 / Greenhouse and garden

Sekrit Theater

The greenhouse and eclectic garden details create a layered, nostalgic setting.

What to wear and try. Choose a simple outfit so the textures have room to show. Compare light through the greenhouse glass with shaded outdoor corners.

When to go. Late afternoon if available, or scout the booked time for light.

Access and planning. Reserve a photography slot directly with the private venue and check current rates and policies. Venue information ↗

10 / Lakeside and easy movement

Mueller Lake Park

Open lawns, water and paths provide several looks within one walk.

What to wear and try. Try casual layers or a breezy solid-color outfit. Start along a path and finish near the water for a softer background.

When to go. Late afternoon into golden hour.

Access and planning. Check park rules before planning a styled setup involving tables, rentals or lighting. Venue information ↗

Before You Set a Date

Pick your two favorite looks, then choose a place that suits both. Confirm parking, walking distance, changing options and venue rules before settling on a date. Check light at the actual session time and agree on a weather backup with your photographer.

Austin Parks lists a $50 two-hour photo permit for posed portraits at Mount Bonnell, Mayfield Park and Auditorium Shores. The city lists a booking window of 4 to 30 days ahead. Confirm the current fee and availability for your date. Austin Parks photo permits ↗
