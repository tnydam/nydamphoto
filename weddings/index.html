Questions

Wedding Photography, Frequently Asked

Do you offer both photography and videography?
Yes. Photography and videography can be planned together on the same timeline, so you're not managing two separate teams asking for the same moments.
How far in advance should we inquire?
Most couples reach out six to eighteen months before their wedding, since dates on popular weekends fill first. If your wedding is sooner, it's still worth checking availability.
Do you require a retainer?
Yes. A 30% retainer reserves your date and collection, with the remaining balance due before the wedding.
Can we book shorter coverage instead of a full day?
Yes. Our Essential collection covers the core hours of the day, while Signature and Wedding Story extend into full-day coverage from getting ready through reception.
How do you approach wedding portraits and direction?
We provide direction when it improves the photograph — particularly for couple portraits, family formals, and wedding-party photographs. Everything else that happens naturally through the day is documented as it happens.
Is an engagement session included?
A complimentary engagement session is included with our Signature and Wedding Story collections, and can be added to Essential. It also means your wedding isn't the first time we're photographing you together.
Do you provide a second photographer?
Yes. A second photographer is included with Signature and Wedding Story. Essential begins with one photographer. Videography and content creation are separate services that can also be added, depending on your wedding and the coverage you want.
Do you photograph Catholic weddings?
Yes, including full Nuptial Mass ceremonies, with care for the liturgy and the church's requirements throughout the day.
How long until we receive our wedding gallery?
You'll receive a few sneak peek images within 24 to 48 hours. Your complete wedding gallery is delivered within 21 days.
Do you offer albums and prints?
Yes. Heirloom albums and fine-art prints are available directly from your gallery, printed by professional labs.
Do you travel for weddings outside Austin?
Yes. We photograph throughout Central Texas and the Hill Country, and we're available for destination weddings as well.
