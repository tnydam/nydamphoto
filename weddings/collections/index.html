    Questions

    Questions About Collections

    How much coverage do we need?
    It depends on your day. Essential includes up to 4 hours and suits intimate weddings and elopements; Signature includes 6 to 8 hours for a full traditional wedding day; and Wedding Story spans 10+ hours across the weekend, including the rehearsal dinner and a next-morning farewell brunch. Use our coverage calculator above for a personalized starting point.
    Do you require a retainer?
    Yes. A 30% retainer secures your date and collection, with the remaining balance due before the wedding.
    Can we customize a collection?
    Yes. Each collection is a starting point. We can add a second photographer, wedding videographer, or wedding content creator, add an engagement session or bridal session to Essential, or include additional events to fit your day.
    Is travel included?
    Travel within Central Texas is included. Destination weddings and travel beyond the area are quoted individually.
    What if our timeline changes?
    Wedding timelines shift, and we plan for that. We'll work with you as details firm up so your coverage still fits the day.
    When do we receive our gallery?
    You'll receive a few wedding sneak peeks within 24 to 48 hours, and your complete gallery within 21 days.
